AI Agents

AI agents that work with your business.

An agent built for one real task — screening, triage, drafting replies — not a general-purpose chatbot.

What it solves

Intelligent agents for real business tasks.

RecruitSynch AI uses Gemini to draft job descriptions and speed up resume screening. Nexus DMS uses Gemini for sales insights from a dealer's own lead and inventory data.

How it works

From understanding to action.

Understand

Reads the input — a resume, a lead, a message — against your criteria.

Decide

Scores, drafts or classifies, staying inside clear guardrails.

Act

Surfaces the result for a human decision, or completes the task directly.
